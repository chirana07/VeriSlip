Frequently Asked Questions

How does VeriSlip detect doctored amounts on bank slips?

VeriSlip combines classical signal processing (Error Level Analysis to detect recompression divergence and Laplacian edge analysis) with a dual-stream neural network that identifies patched font boundaries and spliced pixels.

Can VeriSlip verify slips sent through WhatsApp with compression?

Yes. Social messaging apps re-compress images uniformly. VeriSlip's Layer 3 noise residual filter and Layer 4 feature fusion detect the localized variance break that occurred before transmission.

Is the PDF audit certificate admissible in merchant disputes?

Yes. The certificate includes full pixel coordinates of flagged regions, multi-layer risk breakdowns, and a unique cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprint verifiable against dispute records.

Which Sri Lankan banks are currently supported?

Commercial Bank (ComBank Digital), Sampath Bank (Vishwa), Bank of Ceylon (BOC Digi/SmartPay), Hatton National Bank (SOLO/Digital), Seylan Bank PLC, and Nations Trust Bank (FriMi), alongside generic CEFTS/SLIPS.
